About and sources

Scope and references for this tool.

Show details

The five-kingdom system is a school-level model that is useful for learning basic features of life. Modern taxonomy is based on evolutionary relationships (phylogeny), where Monera and Protista are not natural groups. Scientific names in italics are examples; “e.g.” marks a representative species or genus.

  • Haeckel, E. (1866). Generelle Morphologie der Organismen.
  • Copeland, H. F. (1956). The Classification of Lower Organisms.
  • Whittaker, R. H. (1969). New concepts of kingdoms of organisms. Science, 163(3863), 150–160.
  • Woese, C. R., Kandler, O., & Wheelis, M. L. (1990). Towards a natural system of organisms. PNAS, 87(12), 4576–4579.
